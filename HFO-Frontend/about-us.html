About Us

Who are we?

No Name Kitchen (NNK) is a non-governmental organization dedicated to supporting migrants and refugees across Europe. We work directly on the ground to provide essential aid and defend human rights.

What is our mission?

Our mission is to provide dignity, support, and essential resources to people on the move, while advocating for their rights and raising awareness about their situation.

What do we do?

We set up mobile and fixed kitchens in different locations to prepare and distribute food to people in vulnerable situations. Beyond food, we also provide clothing, hygiene products, and basic support.

Why did we start?

NNK was created in response to the growing humanitarian crisis affecting migrants and refugees. We saw a lack of basic resources and support, and decided to take action by providing immediate, practical help.

Where do we operate?

We operate in various border regions and migration routes across Europe, especially in areas where humanitarian assistance is limited or absent.

What makes us different?

Our approach is direct and hands-on. We work closely with local communities and volunteers, ensuring that aid reaches those who need it most, quickly and efficiently.

Who do we help?

We support migrants, refugees, and displaced people who are facing difficult living conditions, often without access to basic necessities.

How can you get involved?

You can support NNK by volunteering, donating, or helping spread awareness about the situation faced by migrants and refugees.

More information?

Please visit our official website at www.nonamekitchen.org for more information about our work, how to get involved, and how to support our mission.
