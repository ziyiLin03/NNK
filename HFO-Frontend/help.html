Help & Support

Workflow summary

From the menu, located at top left, you can go to “+ New Testimonial” Please select the type of testimonial. Manual and Map reports contain key field data for evaluation and analysis, while audio and photo reports allow you to add supporting evidence.

You are able to either save it as a draft for future modifications (Edit/ Delete) or submit it right away. Once you submit it, you will no longer have access to that testimonial.

What information should be included in a report?

Include key field data such as location, date, route details, and marked points (safe areas, danger areas, pushbacks). Add supporting evidence like photos, audio, and brief testimonies when possible.

Ensure all information is accurate and respects privacy.

What information should NOT be included?

Do not include unnecessary personal identifiers, guesses or sensitive details that are not essential for operational purposes. Always prioritize the safety and privacy of individuals involved.

How many audios can I make in one report?

To avoid overloading the system, a limit of 5 audio recordings per testimonial has been set.

How long can an audio be?

To avoid overloading the system, each audio recording is limited to a maximum duration of 10 minutes.

How many photos can I take in one report?

To avoid overloading the system, a maximum of 10 photos per testimonial has been set.

What happens with saved testimonials that are not submitted?

Draft testimonials will be NOTconsidered for data treatment, we encourage you to submit them or delete them.
